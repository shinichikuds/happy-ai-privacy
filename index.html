Happy AI Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

1. About Happy AI

Happy AI is a personal social-media assistant used to manage photographs and social-media content for a dog named Happy. The application is operated by the owner of the Happy Time social-media Page.

2. Information We Process

Happy AI may process photographs submitted by the authorized user through Telegram for the purpose of analyzing and preparing social-media content.

The application may also process information necessary to authenticate the authorized user and publish approved content to connected social-media platforms.

3. How We Use Information

Submitted photographs may be processed by an artificial intelligence service to:

  • Analyze image quality
  • Generate captions
  • Generate hashtags
  • Suggest social-media posting formats
  • Assist with preparing content for publication

Content is only published to connected social-media Pages after approval by the authorized user.

4. Artificial Intelligence Processing

Happy AI uses an artificial intelligence service to analyze submitted photographs and generate suggested social-media content.

AI-generated content is reviewed by the authorized user before publication.

5. Facebook and Meta

Happy AI may use Meta's APIs to publish approved photographs and accompanying text to the Happy Time Facebook Page.

Happy AI does not publish content to the Facebook Page without an explicit approval action by the authorized user.

6. Telegram

Telegram is used as the user interface for submitting photographs, reviewing AI-generated content, approving content, editing captions, and initiating publication.

7. Storage

Submitted photographs and related content may be temporarily stored on the computer running Happy AI for processing, record keeping, and social-media publishing.

8. Data Sharing

Information submitted to Happy AI may be transmitted to third-party services required to provide the application's functionality, including artificial intelligence processing and social-media publishing.

Happy AI does not sell personal information.

9. Data Retention

Photographs and generated content may be retained for as long as necessary to operate the application. The application owner may delete stored photographs and related records.

10. Data Deletion Requests

To request deletion of information processed by Happy AI, contact the application owner using the contact information associated with this application.

Requests may include deletion of submitted photographs, generated captions, and related application records.

11. Security

Authentication credentials and API credentials are intended to be stored securely and are not intentionally published through the application.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the functionality of Happy AI changes. The latest version will be published on this page.

13. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the owner of the Happy AI application through the contact information associated with the application.
